# Where there is smoke, there is fire.

**Type:** Saying  
**Source:** Manabí, beliefs and customs  
**Theme:** Popular wisdom

---

## Meaning

This proverb teaches that every rumor or sign usually has a real cause behind it. It reflects the wisdom of observation and experience in Manabí's culture.

---

## Cultural Relevance

Used to warn or advise about the importance of paying attention to signs and not ignoring possible problems.
